# Runbook: verifying a provider

Every provider is checked by a person before they can see requests. The console records what you checked, and every decision is audit-logged.

## Where

`/admin/providers` (admins only). The Pending tab is oldest first. Aim to review within one working day.

## Verification levels

| Level | Meaning         | Minimum to reach it                                        |
| ----- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0     | Nothing checked |                                                            |
| 1     | Phone confirmed | A call where you confirm you are speaking to the applicant |
| 2     | ID checked      | Government ID seen and matching their name and photo       |
| 3     | Met in person   | You met them and saw their tools or real work              |

Approval needs at least level 1, a phone number on file (the provider gives it in their application), and at least one passed check. In the wedge, aim for level 3 for every provider: it is our strongest early trust signal.

## Steps

1. Open the applicant. Read the headline, about text and area.
2. Call the number shown (tap to call). It was typed by the provider and is not verified until you reach them. Confirm their name, what they do and where they work. Watch for answers that do not match the application.
3. If you can, see a government ID (in person or a clear video call) and their tools or past work.
4. Tick every check that passed. Add internal notes (not shown to the provider).
5. Choose the verification level that matches what you actually checked. Never round up.
6. Decide:
   - **Approve**: the checks passed.
   - **Reject**: write a specific, kind reason they will see (for example "Please add what services you offer and your years of experience"). They can fix it and resubmit.
   - **Suspend**: for an approved provider with serious complaints. Write the reason they will see.

## Red flags

- The person on the phone is not the applicant, or several applicants share one phone number.
- Asking customers to pay before a job is agreed, or asking for payment outside what is quoted.
- Refusing any form of ID for level 2 or above.

When in doubt, reject with a clear reason, or leave the application pending and ask the founder.

## Data protection

Phone numbers are shown only in this console, to call applicants. Do not copy them into chats, spreadsheets or personal phones. Never store ID document photos outside the private verification bucket (later phase).
